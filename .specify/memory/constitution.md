## Core Principles

### I. Elegant & Timeless Design
The website and e-store MUST reflect the brand's premium, classic identity. The "parchment" theme, inspired by the original design, MUST be tastefully integrated to create a unique and memorable user experience.

### II. High-Quality Product Showcase
As a premium stitching unit, the products on the e-store MUST be presented with high-resolution photos, detailed descriptions, and clear specifications. The goal is to digitally replicate the tangible quality of the garments.

### III. Seamless User Journey
The user experience, from browsing the product catalog to completing a purchase, MUST be smooth, intuitive, and efficient. The e-store should be easy to navigate, with a clear path to checkout.

### IV. Secure & Trustworthy Transactions
The e-store MUST use secure payment gateways and be transparent about shipping and return policies to build customer trust. All customer data MUST be handled with the utmost confidentiality and in compliance with privacy regulations.

### V. Performance & Accessibility
The site MUST be fast, responsive, and accessible to all users, regardless of their device or abilities. A slow or inaccessible website is not a premium experience.

### VI. Clear Communication
Contact information, quotes, and customer service channels MUST be easy to find and use. This ensures that customers can easily get in touch for inquiries, support, or to commission custom work.

## Development Workflow

All development work, from minor updates to new features, MUST be documented in a specification document before implementation. This includes a clear description of the change, the rationale behind it, and any potential impact on other parts of the system.

## Governance

This constitution is the single source of truth for the project's principles and guidelines. Any changes to this constitution must be reviewed and approved by the project owner. All development and content creation must align with this constitution.
